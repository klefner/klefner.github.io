How to Play

The goal

Your team (purple) attacks up the field toward the top end zone. Score touchdowns (7 points). Most points after four 3-minute quarters wins. Get tackled in your own end zone and it's a safety: 2 points for the other team. Zombies can't kick, so there are no field goals.

The brain (there are no downs)

Every possession starts with a FRESH brain. It rots one stage each play: FRESH → FUNKY → RANCID → PUTRID.

Cross midfield and you get a fresh brain from the cooler. Then you have 4 more plays to score. If a play with a Putrid brain ends without crossing midfield or scoring, the brain BURSTS: turnover at that spot.

Limbs

LostEffect
1 armWorse catching and throwing. A tackle that rips off the ball carrier's arm can cause a fumble.
2 armsCan't catch or throw. Still carries the brain in his teeth.
1 legAbout 40% slower. Hops.
2 legsCrawls.
HeadOut for the rest of the drive. A fresh substitute comes in on the next possession.

At halftime, the Stitch-Up sews every limb back on.

Body types

Every zombie is a little different: some are quicker than others, and any of them can stumble mid-run. A zombie missing a leg stumbles a lot more often.

Shamblers (linemen): slow and very tough. Fresh Ones (running back, receivers): fast and fragile. Brainiac (quarterback): best arm, fragile.

Plays

OffenseWhat it does
1 · Shamble DiveHand-off to the running back up the middle.
2 · SlantReceivers cut toward the middle. Quick, short throws.
3 · ScreenShort toss to the running back behind blockers. Beats the Blitz.
4 · Hail MummyBoth receivers go deep. Big gain or big trouble.
Defense
1 · BlitzRush the quarterback with almost everyone. Leaves the running back open.
2 · ZoneDefenders guard areas of the field. Good against deep passes.
3 · ManEach defender shadows one receiver. Solid all-around.

Touch controls (iPhone)

Lower-left of screenPut your thumb down anywhere and slide to run. The farther you slide, the faster you go.
Yellow Q · E · VThrow to that receiver (only shown while your quarterback can pass)
Big yellow buttonStiff-arm when you carry the ball, dive tackle on defense
BURST · SWITCH / TAKE OVER · IISpeed burst once per play · switch defender, or take over your ball carrier · pause

The player you control has a yellow ring. When your quarterback's ring flashes red, a rusher is about to hit him: throw now. After you throw, your receiver plays himself: he runs to the X where the brain will land, hustles if the throw is ahead of him, and dives if he's just short. After the catch he keeps running on his own; press Tab (iPhone: TAKE OVER) whenever you want to steer him.
