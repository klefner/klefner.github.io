How to Play

Quick start

  • Pick a play, then move your player (yellow ring) with the stick, WASD or by holding the mouse.
  • THROW (Space, or the big button) passes to the receiver marked OPEN. You can also tap or click any receiver.
  • Gain 4 yards every play or the defense takes a bite of your brain. Three bites is a turnover.
  • On defense, DIVE (Space) to tackle. Keep holding a direction to steer the dive on the way. You switch to whoever is closest to the ball.

Your first game coaches you through the first few plays. Want every receiver on its own key and to switch players yourself? Set Controls to Pro in Settings.

The goal

Your team (purple) attacks up the field toward the top end zone. Score touchdowns (7 points). Most points when time runs out wins: two 3-minute halves in a Quick Match, four 3-minute quarters in a Classic Match. Get tackled in your own end zone and it's a safety: 2 points for the other team. Zombies can't kick, so there are no field goals.

Dead Time: tied when time runs out? The game goes to Dead Time. A coin toss decides who gets a fresh brain at midfield, the clock keeps resetting, and the next score wins. The losing team dies right there on the field, each zombie in their own special way, and the bats clear away what's left. Tap to skip it.

Three Bites (there are no downs)

Gain 4 yards or the defense takes a bite of your brain. Three bites and they eat it.

Every possession starts with a whole brain. Each play, reach the green bite line 4 yards ahead (3 on Easy, 5 on Hard) and the brain is safe. Fall short, throw an incompletion or get sacked and the defense takes a bite. A gain of 15 yards or more heals one bite.

You can see it happen: the brain at the top of the screen and in your runner's hands loses a chunk with every bite and turns greener, and a defender chomps it on the field.

The third bite is a turnover at that spot: BRAIN EATEN! The defense devours it, so there's no brain left to score with, and a ghost brings a fresh brain for the other team. With two bites taken you can call Head Punt instead of a play: the team lines up, the brain is snapped, and your quarterback heads it sky-high (zombies can't kick). It splats down 35 yards away and the other team starts there. Tap to skip it.

Body types

Every zombie is a little different: some are quicker than others, and any of them can stumble mid-run. A zombie missing a leg stumbles a lot more often.

Shamblers (linemen): slow and very tough. Fresh Ones (running back, receivers): fast and fragile. Brainiac (quarterback): best arm, fragile.

Season

Pick Season on the title screen to coach the Graveyard Shift through a 12-team zombie league: 12 weekly games, standings in two divisions, and rival teams whose managers get stronger every week. Every game pays Bones (100 for a win, 30 for a loss, 10 per touchdown). Your career saves itself after every game.

The playoffs: after week 12 the top four in each division go on. In the Wild Card round 1st hosts 4th and 2nd hosts 3rd, the winners meet in the Graveyard Championship, and the two division champions play the Rot Bowl. Playoff wins pay big: 150 Bones in the Wild Card, 250 in the Championship and 500 for the Rot Bowl. Miss out, or get knocked out, and you can watch the rest play out or skip straight to the winner. The Graveyard gets a fresh shelf before every playoff round.

Roster & Training: each zombie has skulls in Speed, Hands, Bite and Grit (plus Arm for a quarterback). Every skull you buy really changes how that zombie runs, catches, tackles, holds together or throws, and raises that zombie's Player Power. Your seven starters' Power makes your Team Power, with the quarterback counting extra.

The Graveyard: walk the graveyard at night. It starts out a pleasant place and gets nastier as the season goes on. Every headstone carries the name of a fresh corpse. Hover a headstone (or tap it) to see that zombie's Power, skulls, price and where it would play, then double-click (or double-tap) the dirt in front of it to dig it up. It climbs out, the bats dress it in your uniform, and it starts if it beats your weakest zombie at its spot, or joins the bench if not. Brainiacs play QB and Safety, Fresh corpses play running back, receiver, linebacker and corner, and Shamblers play the lines. Up to three zombies sit on the bench, and when a starter is knocked out the next one comes in. With no bench left, your team plays a zombie short until halftime. Letting a zombie go pays back 20% of what it's worth.

Timeouts and the Coffin Corner: in a Season game, press Esc between plays to call a timeout. Open the Coffin Corner to sit any zombie on the field down and send in one from the bench. A zombie you pull early keeps only the parts it has already lost, so it costs less to stitch up after the game, and you can send it back in later. A zombie that was knocked out can't come back until halftime. Timeouts are free and unlimited.

Deaths, the Stitch Shop and lifespan: a zombie knocked out in a game has died. It plays again next week, but each death costs a little Power for good. Parts that fly off stay off after the game and cost Power until the Stitch Shop sews them back on. Once your spending opens it, the Witch Doctor can erase the Power lost in a zombie's latest death. Every zombie also has a lifespan: deaths, stitches and each season wear it down, and better zombies last longer. When it runs out, you choose: Keep it (weaker, and no more training), retire it to the Mausoleum, your hall of fame, or the Sacrifice, which fires your team up for the next game.

The Altar: walk right from the graves (the signpost, or → past the last grave) to the Altar, where Madame Morgue buys zombies for Bones. Pick a zombie from the line, then a station: the Spider's Loom, the Cauldron, the Bone Mill or the Trapdoor. A spry, healthy zombie pays more than a worn-out has-been, free Z-team walk-ons pay nothing, and your team plays fired up next game. A zombie whose time is up can go straight to the Altar from its Time's up screen.
